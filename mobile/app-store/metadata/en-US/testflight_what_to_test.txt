Welcome to the PhoneHome beta — the home phone, reborn for families.

What's new in 1.0.1 — calls
• Fixed a crash that could end a call with a "Call Failed" screen when either side hung up
• Hold and hang-up from the house phone now reach a locked iPhone right away

Also recent — smoother launch
• Opening PhoneHome now shows the ringing rotary phone, then the speed dial animates in — no more Sign in flash or flicker
• Prices and promo codes live on our website only; the app itself stays free
• Privacy, Terms, and Support links in Parent settings go to the PhoneHome website
• Welcome: switch to a different Mothership in one tap

GETTING STARTED
1. Install from TestFlight, then open PhoneHome.
2. Splash → How it works → Connect — paste your invite (the whole text message is fine) or your Mothership's https:// address.
3. Sign in, then allow microphone on the first call.

PLEASE TEST
