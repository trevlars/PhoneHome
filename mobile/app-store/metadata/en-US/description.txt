PhoneHome is the home phone, reborn: a private, parent-controlled voice network for kids and extended family.

YOUR PEOPLE ONLY
• Mutual allowlist — both sides approve before anyone rings
• 6-digit invite codes for cousins; no VPN or public phone number
• Kid-friendly speed dial, TreeHouse hangout rooms, and grandparent-simple mode

PARENT CONTROLS
• Quiet Time (Bedtime, School, custom schedules + US holiday skip)
• Daily talk cap and household Timeout (pause all calls)
• Parent PIN, Activity log, and co-parent invites

REAL HOUSE PHONES
• Optional Grandstream HT801 analog adapter (SIP TLS + SRTP)
• Same family rules for app and wall phone

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Softphones use HTTPS + WebRTC (DTLS-SRTP)
• Analog uses encrypted SIP TLS + SRTP — not cleartext public SIP
• No call recording by default; you host your own Mothership server

PhoneHome is inspired by the open Digits project (AGPL). Most families join someone else's Mothership as a new Pod; tech-savvy relatives can self-host.
