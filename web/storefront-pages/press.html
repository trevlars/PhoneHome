The home phone, reborn. Private, encrypted family calling — kids tap a face to call people parents approve. Not a carrier; no PSTN or 911.

Key facts

  • Host price: $25/year after 30-day trial; families who join pay $0
  • Capacity: up to 50 phones per license
  • Privacy: no ads, no call recording, no storefront tracking pixels
  • Encryption: WebRTC DTLS-SRTP (apps); SIP TLS + SRTP (house phones)
  • Seller: the operator
