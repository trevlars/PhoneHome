How the 30 days work

  1. Install and boot On a Mac, PC, Raspberry Pi, Home Assistant, or Docker. The clock starts now.
  2. Invite your family Send links to kids, co-parents, grandparents, and friends. Approve who can call whom.
  3. Try a house phone (optional) Plug any RJ11 phone into a ~$40 Grandstream HT801 and let the kids ring grandma.
  4. Decide by day 30 Buy the $25/year host license and paste it in — or don’t. Calls pause, your data stays put, and nothing is charged.

Private from day one

During your trial, PhoneHome the company sees:

  • Your calls: nothing — they stay on your Mothership, encrypted end to end between your phones and it
  • Your contacts and call history: nothing
  • Your location: nothing — the app never asks
  • Ads or tracking: none, ever
  • A central database to breach: there isn’t one — each Mothership holds only its own circle

The one exception: to make an iPhone ring, a caller’s display name passes through our push relay and isn’t stored. Read what we never keep.

Why families spin up a Mothership

  • You hold the data. Home Assistant, Docker, or a spare PC — the family’s calls live on your box, not a kid-phone cloud. No giant pool of families means no giant breach — why that matters.
  • Any handset. Western Electric, Princess phone, thrift-store cordless, or the free app. No proprietary plastic phone required.
  • One license, whole circle. Up to 50 phones for $25/year — not a new subscription per wrist or per Can.
  • Friends can host too. Cousins can run their own Mothership and still call yours once both hosts complete a peer invite.

Trial FAQ

How does the free trial work?

Install the Mothership on a computer that stays on, and the 30-day clock starts on first boot — not on download day. Every feature is unlocked. No card is required, and nothing renews automatically.

What happens when the 30 days are up?

If you buy the $25/year license, paste it in and nothing changes. If you don’t, calls pause until you activate — you can still sign in, change settings, or paste a license later. Your family’s data stays on your machine, and you can delete it whenever you like.

Do grandparents and other families need their own trial?

No. Only the person hosting the Mothership installs anything. Everyone else gets an invite link and uses the free app or a house phone — they never pay.

Do I need special kid phones?

No. Use the free app, or plug nearly any analog RJ11 phone into a ~$40 adapter. Cool old handsets and thrift finds are encouraged.

Where is family call data stored?

On your Mothership. PhoneHome sells the license and runs a push relay so iPhones can ring — it never keeps your calls, contacts, or call history.

Can I move the Mothership to another computer later?

Yes. PhoneHome has a move flow for leaving one Mothership and setting up another, and support can re-issue a license to new hardware.
