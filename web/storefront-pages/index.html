Parent tools

Apps use WebRTC with DTLS-SRTP. The push relay only tells Apple an iPhone should ring — it stores none of it. Privacy policy.

How it nests

One Mothership. Every family you invite.

The Mothership is a small server you — or someone you trust — leave running. It hosts the families. You don’t rent a phone line, and joining families never see each other’s phone books.

  1. MothershipThe computer that stays on. Mac, PC, Pi, Home Assistant, or Docker.
  2. PodA family name. North. East. West. Parents run that Pod.
  3. CircleOne child, and only the people their guardians allow them to call.
  • 01
    Invite a sister or in-lawThey join your Pod as a parent and add their own kids. Never invite a child alone.
  • 02
    Invite Grandma and GrandpaA simpler phone: big buttons, one tap, the grandchildren you chose.
  • 03
    Invite one kid’s friendThat friend’s parent lands in one child’s Circle — not your whole family phone book.
  • 04
    Up to 50 phonesApps and house phones, as many families as you like, on the one license.

The code is public on GitHub. The license pays for installers, updates, and the push relay.

Straight answers

Who pays?

Only the person who hosts the Mothership. It’s free for 30 days from first boot, then $25 a year for up to 50 phones. Kids, grandparents, and other families who join pay nothing.

What do I actually install?

Joining: the free iPhone app, or a browser, plus the invite your family sent. Hosting: the Mothership on a Mac, Windows PC, Linux machine, Raspberry Pi, Home Assistant, or Docker. The trial clock starts when you first boot it, not when you download it. All the downloads.

Do kids need a special phone?

No. The app is enough. A house phone is optional: any regular RJ11 phone into a Grandstream HT801, about $40. Corded, cordless, and thrift-store sets all work. It is not a substitute for 911.

Where do calls and contacts live?

On your Mothership. PhoneHome sells the license and runs a push relay so iPhones can ring. It does not keep call audio, contacts, or call history. No recording, no ads, no location tracking. The full policy.

Can it call regular numbers?

No. This is a private family network, not a carrier. It does not call the public phone network or 911. Keep a real cell or landline for emergencies.

Already have an invite?

You don’t need this trial. Open the link they sent, or get the iPhone app and paste their Mothership address.
