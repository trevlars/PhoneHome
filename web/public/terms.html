What you buy

  • A signed host license for one Mothership instance (one private family hub), renewable yearly.

  • Softphone clients (web / free iOS app) remain free; they connect to your hub.

What PhoneHome is not

  • Not a telephone company. Stage 1 does not connect to the public switched telephone network (PSTN) and cannot place carrier 911 / emergency calls.
  • Not a substitute for emergency service. House phones on an analog adapter that dial 911 may ring a parent in the household — not a public-safety answering point. Keep a real phone or cell for emergencies.
  • Not advertising-supported; we do not sell call content or mine conversations.
