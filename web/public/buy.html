License FAQ

What does the $25/year license cover?

One Mothership with up to 50 phones — apps and house phones — shared by any number of families. It includes easy-install updates and the push relay that makes iPhones ring. The app is free; house-phone adapters are bring-your-own.

Do families who join my Mothership pay anything?

No. Only the host buys a license. Grandparents, cousins, and friends who join you pay $0.

Does it renew automatically?

Yes, once a year through Stripe, at $25. You can cancel any time before the next billing date by emailing support or using the Stripe customer portal.

What happens if I stop paying?

Your Mothership keeps working for a 30-day grace period after the paid year ends. Then calls pause until you renew. Sign-in, settings, and your family’s data all stay on your machine.

Can I get a refund?

Yes — within 14 days of purchase if you bought by mistake or it won’t run on your hardware after a good-faith try. Email support@example.com with your receipt. Details: Refunds & cancellation.

Can I move my license to new hardware?

Yes. A license belongs to one Mothership at a time; email support and we’ll re-issue it for the new one.

Is payment inside the iPhone app?

No. Checkout is Stripe on this website, and the iPhone app stays free.
