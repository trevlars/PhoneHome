Apps for the family

iPhone & iPad app — free

Lock-screen answering and Face ID. Now in public beta: get it on the App Store. Paste your Mothership address or an invite link to connect.

Android app — internal test

Join with the Google account on the phone: Play internal testing opt-in. After you accept, open the Play Store app (not a browser store link) → profile → Manage apps & device → Testing → install PhoneHome. Until Google finishes first review, “Download test app” on the web can 404 — the Play Store app path still works for invited testers.

What the Mothership talks to

Your calls never leave it. Only two things reach PhoneHome:

  • License check: done on your own machine with a signed license — no account lookup
  • iPhone ringing: a call ID and caller name pass through our push relay to Apple, and aren’t stored
  • Everything else: calls, contacts, call history, and voicemail stay on your Mothership

What stays on the Mothership is spelled out in the privacy policy.

Downloads FAQ

Which download is the free trial?

All of them. Every Mothership download starts a 30-day free trial on first boot. Buy a $25/year license later and paste it into the same install — no reinstall.

What does a family member who is joining need?

Nothing from this page. They use the invite link, the free iPhone app, or a browser. Only the host downloads a Mothership.

Is there an Intel Mac version?

Not yet — Intel Macs can build from source for now. Apple Silicon Macs use the menu bar app.

Does the Mothership phone home to PhoneHome?

Only for two things: it checks your signed license on your own machine (no account lookup), and it uses our push relay so iPhones can ring. Calls, contacts, and call history never leave it.

How do I know what I’m installing?

Mac builds signed with our Developer ID are notarized by Apple. Installers are the builds published for the host license.
